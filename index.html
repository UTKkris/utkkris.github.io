Intent-Aware Anomaly Detector

Signature firewalls only catch attacks somebody has already named. This one learns what your traffic normally looks like and blocks the flows that do not fit, then writes the firewall rule itself.

live flow classifier0 flows

    A simulated log showing network flows being classified as allowed or blocked in real time.

    The problem

    A botnet that rotates its payload walks straight past a signature-based rule set. The behaviour, though, stays recognisable: the timing, the burst shape, the jitter between packets. That is what I trained on instead of the payload.

    What I built

    A stateful flow manager reassembles live packets with Scapy and computes statistical features such as inter-arrival time, jitter and burstiness at millisecond latency. Those go into LightGBM and Random Forest models trained on the CIC-IDS-2018 dataset (16M records). When a flow scores as hostile, an automated response pipeline pushes a rule through Windows Netsh, scoped narrowly enough that legitimate traffic on the same host keeps flowing.

    Known limits

    Rules go through Netsh so it is Windows-only today; an nftables backend is the obvious next step. Accuracy on a curated 2018 dataset is not the same as accuracy on live traffic. I would want a false-positive budget measured on real capture before trusting it unattended.

    LinkedIn Content Shield

    People post their offer letters on LinkedIn with the salary, the address and the signature still visible. This strips all of it in the browser, before the file ever leaves the machine.

    offer_letter.pdfunprotected

    Dear Utkarsh Shukla, we are pleased to confirm your appointment at a gross annual compensation of ₹18,40,000.

    Kindly verify using PAN ABCDE1234F and the address on record: Flat 402, Road 12, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad 500034.

    Regards, A. Mehta, Head of People

    5 high-risk entities found on this page

    The problem

    Every upload tool that redacts documents does it on a server, which means the unredacted file gets transmitted first. For the exact documents people most want to redact, that is the wrong trade.

    What I built

    Entity extraction runs entirely in the browser using WebAssembly and a local OCR pass, flagging compensation figures, national IDs, addresses and signature blocks. An interactive preview lets you confirm each hit, then the export flattens the document and burns out the vector text layer, so the black bar is not something you can select and copy back out. No server round trip at any point.

    Field research · CFSL Hyderabad · 2025

    What people actually click

    At the Central Forensic Science Laboratory I cloned a banking login and ran a controlled phishing simulation, then read the behaviour logs to work out where detection should sit.

    The setup

    A clone of a banking portal, instrumented to log every interaction: time to first click, hesitation before submitting, whether anyone checked the certificate. Run inside the lab, on consenting participants, as part of a wider forensics engagement.

    What I found

    It is surprisingly easy to craft a malicious phishing link and how prone the people are to these scams if they aren't proactive in their measures.

    Alongside this I worked live investigations at CFSL, from evidence acquisition through to the written report, which is where I learned that the hard part of forensics is chain of custody, not tooling.

    How I work

    Read the system first. Guess later.

    My work sits where C++ and Python meet network security. Most of what I build comes from the same instinct: watch how a system behaves, then work backward to figure out the model underneath it.

    That has meant training on sixteen million traffic records to tell a botnet from a person, cloning a bank login in a forensics lab to study how victims actually behave, and hardening authentication layers on a production SaaS backend. Different surfaces, same question: what is this system really doing, versus what it claims to do?

    The moment a mess of constraints suddenly clicks into one clean idea is the reason I do this.
